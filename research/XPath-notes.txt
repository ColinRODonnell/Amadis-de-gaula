XPath Notes - Colin O'Donnell
#####################

*Note* these are slightly different than XPath for XML. 

Count paragraphs --> count(//*:p)
in Southey_amadis_1803_2.xml it counts 10 <p>

Finds Person Name --> //*:persName

Finds Place Name --> //*:placeName

Counts Starting "Anchors" --> count(//*:anchor[@ana="start"])
Counts Ending "Anchors" --> count(//*:anchor[@ana="end"])

If I think of any more I will put them here. 
Most of these are specifically useful for me because
I am taking a completely bare document and modeling it 
from previous work. - CRO
